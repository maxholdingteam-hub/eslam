# Monetization Economics and YouTube Policies for an Arabic Kids Cartoon Channel

Research date: 13 Nov 2026. Most data is from 2025-2026; older items are flagged. Most RPM/CPM figures come from third-party creator-tool vendors, not YouTube. YouTube does not publish RPM by country, language or niche.

## 1. RPM/CPM: Made for Kids vs general; Arabic audiences vs US; Shorts

### Takeaway
"Made for Kids" (MFK) long-form content has the lowest RPM of any niche in the best available dataset: a median of about $0.33 per 1,000 views (P25-P75 $0.16-$0.88), against an all-niche median of $2.30. Gulf audiences (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait) pay several times more than Egypt and the Levant. Shorts pay roughly 3-14% of long-form RPM. In practice, 1M MFK views in Arabic will likely earn in the hundreds of dollars, not the thousands.

### Cited Findings
- AIR Media-Tech study: 300 channels, 3,595 monetized channel-months, May 2025-May 2026, using YouTube Studio data. Kids & Teens long-form median RPM is $0.33 (P25-P75 $0.16-$0.88), the lowest of 13 niches. The all-niche median is $2.30. Education & Science is $10.22, Entertainment $2.43, Music $2.28. The study blames the low kids figure on the lack of personalized ads for MFK content. — [AIR Media-Tech, YouTube Monetization Statistics 2026](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-monetization-statistics-2026)
- The same study gives a median Studio CPM of $1.59 for Kids & Teens, versus $5.91 for Education & Science. — [AIR Media-Tech (via search summary)](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/how-much-does-youtube-really-pay-in-2026-real-rpm-data-from-300-channels)
- Other, looser estimates for MFK: CPM $1-$3 and RPM about $0.55-$1.65 (vidIQ-type guides). These do not match AIR's measured data. — [vidIQ, Made for Kids: How to Make Money 2026](https://www.vidiq.com/blog/post/make-money-kids-youtube-channel)
- Shorts data from AIR (274 channels, 12 months): Shorts RPM is 3-14% of long-form RPM in nearly every niche, ranging from $0.02 to $1.48 per 1,000 views. Most channels needed 11,000-34,000 Shorts views to match the revenue of 1,000 long-form views. Shorts were under 2% of total revenue for most channels. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-monetization-statistics-2026)
- Several 2026 guides put average Shorts RPM at about $0.01-$0.07 per 1,000 views. Treat these as low-quality estimates. — [Travelpayouts](https://travelpayouts.com/blog/youtube-cpm-rates/); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=116686)
- Official Shorts revenue model: Shorts Feed ad revenue is pooled and allocated to a Creator Pool by engaged views, per country. Creators keep 45% of their allocated revenue. Music use reduces the pool's share for that Short, but not the creator's 45% rate. — [YouTube Help: Shorts monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- Advertiser CPM by audience country (AIR, published June 2025, 3,000+ channels): US $14.67, Australia $13.30, India $0.74, Yemen $0.48. This is before YouTube's share, so RPM is lower. The study has no Arabic or MENA breakdown beyond Yemen. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-monetization-statistics-2026)
- Unsourced vendor estimates of MENA RPM (FluxNote, March 2026, no methodology, and internally inconsistent): Qatar $5-12, UAE $4-10, Saudi $3-8, Kuwait $3-8, Egypt $0.5-2, Syria/Lebanon $1-3, Iraq $0.5-2. The same page says kids content cuts CPM by about 50%. — [FluxNote, YouTube RPM Middle East 2026](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-middle-east-rpm-2026)
- Other estimates conflict with these. For example, one blog gives UAE CPM $2.5-6, Saudi $2-5 and Egypt $0.15-0.80 (older-style data). — [thesrzone (flag: low quality, possibly older)](https://www.thesrzone.com/2024/01/youtube-rpm-rates-by-country.html); [FluxNote ME CPM](https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-creators-middle-east-2026)
- Audience location matters more than creator location. Over 95% of watch time on UAE-based channels and over 60% on Egyptian channels comes from outside the country (YouTube data as of Dec 2024, published April 2025). — [Net Influencer, YouTube 20th anniversary MENA](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-reports-record-growth-in-mena-creator-earnings-on-20th-anniversary/)

### Inferences
- Since MENA general RPMs are below US levels and MFK cuts RPM sharply, a realistic planning range for an Arabic MFK cartoon is about $0.15-$1.00 RPM long-form. A Gulf-heavy audience would sit at the upper end, and Egypt, Iraq, Algeria or Morocco-heavy audiences at the lower end. Arabic kids channels typically get large shares of views from Egypt, Iraq and North Africa, which pushes the blended figure down. This is an inference, not measured.
- Shorts will add reach but negligible revenue: about $0.01-$0.10 per 1,000 views for kids content, most likely.
- Longer videos (8+ minutes, which allow mid-rolls) and compilations are the usual levers for RPM per view. I did not verify this against a source in this session.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable, measured RPM data specific to Arabic-language MFK content or to the Saudi, UAE or Egypt kids audiences. All country figures are vendor estimates.
- I did not retrieve an official YouTube source for the long-form 55% creator share in this session. One vendor guide states it ("RPM is ~55% of CPM"), but it should be confirmed with YouTube's YPP terms.

## 2. Effects of COPPA / "Made for Kids" designation

### Takeaway
MFK videos keep ad monetization through contextual, non-personalized ads only. They lose comments, the notification bell, memberships, merch, Super Chat, cards and end screens, miniplayer, and save-to-playlist. This removes most engagement and fan-funding tools and lowers RPM. The amended US COPPA Rule (in force from June 2025, compliance deadline 22 April 2026) raises the legal stakes.

### Cited Findings
- Features disabled on a video set as MFK: autoplay on home, cards and end screens, video watermarks, channel memberships, comments, donate button, likes and dislikes on YouTube Music, live chat and live chat donations, merchandise and ticketing, notification bell, personalized advertising, playback in the miniplayer, Super Chat and Super Stickers, and Save to playlist / Save to watch later. — [YouTube Help: Set your channel or video's audience](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654)
- If the whole channel is set as MFK, channel memberships, the notification bell, Posts and Ask Studio are also unavailable. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654)
- YouTube itself says "Not serving personalized ads on kids content may result in a decrease in revenue." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654)
- MFK videos are more likely to be recommended alongside other kids' videos. They are not automatically included in the YouTube Kids app. — [YouTube Help: Made for kids FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9684541)
- Ads on MFK content must not incite children to buy or urge parents to buy. Personalized ads and third-party trackers are barred. All food and beverage ads around MFK content on YouTube main have been banned since January 2020 (an older rule, still referenced). — [Google Ads policy: Ads & made for kids content](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9683742); [WFA, Jan 2020](https://wfanet.org/knowledge/item/2020/01/10/YouTube-prohibits-all-food-and-beverage-ads-next-to-kids-content-as-of-today)
- FTC amended COPPA Rule: published in the Federal Register 22 April 2025, effective 23 June 2025, with most compliance required by 22 April 2026. — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [Hunton](https://hunton.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/ftc-publishes-final-coppa-rule-amendments)
- In February 2026 the FTC issued a policy statement limiting COPPA enforcement for data collected for age verification. It continues to signal robust enforcement. — [Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/ftc-prioritizes-coppa-enforcement-new-compliance-obligations-take-effect)

### Inferences
- An Arabic cartoon aimed at children under 13 must be set as MFK regardless of where the creator is based. YouTube applies the designation globally and the FTC can treat individual channel owners as operators. Mislabeling risks penalties and policy action.
- With no comments, bell, memberships or merch shelf, community building and off-platform revenue have to happen elsewhere: parent-targeted social accounts, an app, or a website.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the FTC's per-violation civil penalty amount in this session (2019's $170M Google/YouTube settlement is well known, but I did not re-source it here).
- Arab national rules on child privacy (e.g., Saudi PDPL, UAE child digital safety law) were not researched.

## 3. YouTube Partner Program requirements (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Ad revenue requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. MFK channels can join YPP, but they are judged against the kids quality principles. New YPP terms take effect on 1 February 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Ad-revenue YPP threshold: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months, or 10M valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Shorts Feed views do not count toward watch hours. — [YouTube Help: YPP overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- YPP terms are being updated from 1 February 2027. Creators must accept the new terms in Studio by 31 January 2027. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851)
- If a channel has MFK content, YouTube uses its quality principles for kids and family content to decide monetization status. — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Shorts revenue share: creators keep 45% of allocated Creator Pool revenue. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)

### Inferences
- The lower "fan funding" tier (500 subs / 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views, widely reported since 2023) is of little use to MFK channels, because memberships, Super Chat and Super Thanks-style tools are disabled on MFK content. I did not confirm this tier's thresholds on the official page in this session.

### Gaps
- The content of the Feb 2027 YPP terms change was not described on the page retrieved.

## 4. Inauthentic content (July 2025), AI and mass-produced content, synthetic disclosure, demonetization cases, YouTube Kids quality principles

### Takeaway
From 15 July 2025, the "repetitious content" policy was renamed "inauthentic content". It explicitly names "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production" and "templated storylines" as non-monetizable, and enforcement can remove monetization from a whole channel. For MFK channels, the kids quality principles apply on top of this. A channel with a "strong focus on low-quality made-for-kids content" can be suspended from YPP. AI-generated kids channels have been demonetized and terminated, mostly after press investigations in 2025-2026. Some hand-animated channels have also reported false positives.

### Cited Findings
- Policy text: renamed from "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" on 15 July 2025. Not allowed: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production", "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative, commentary, or educational value", and channels that "heavily rely on generic templates or emotionally manipulative themes". Allowed: the same intro and outro if the bulk of the content differs, and "using AI to edit your video scripts or generate a unique background visual". With templates, "the final product must still demonstrate your creative vision". — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Reused content (copying from other sources without substantive modification, or content uploaded many times by other creators) is separately ineligible, and is distinct from copyright enforcement. — [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- YouTube described the July 2025 change as a minor clarification of existing rules, but said "if you have videos that violate our guidelines, monetization may be removed from your entire channel." — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-monetization-update-spam-not-reaction-channels/550755/)
- YouTube's quality principles for kids and family content:
  - High-quality content promotes looking after yourself and others; learning and curiosity; creativity, play and imagination; life skills; and showing kids the world.
  - Low-quality content is heavily promotional (e.g., unboxing), encourages negative behaviors, is deceptively educational, is hard to follow (jumbled storylines, unclear audio, no beginning, middle and end), is sensational or misleading (including keyword stuffing), or makes strange use of children's characters.
  - High-quality content is raised in recommendations, and the principles also guide YouTube Kids inclusion. Low-quality videos may get limited or no ads, and a channel with a strong focus on low quality may be suspended from YPP.
  — [YouTube Help: Quality principles for kids & family content](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223)
- Altered or synthetic content disclosure: creators must disclose realistic altered or synthetic content through Studio. YouTube may apply labels itself. Clearly unrealistic content such as animation generally does not need a label. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content)
- A WIRED report says animations made for kids are effectively exempt from the deepfake disclosure rules. A YouTube spokesperson said kids creators must still disclose realistic synthetic content. — [WIRED (syndicated)](https://wirednews.pages.dev/story/kids-cartoons-free-pass-youtube-deepfake-disclosure-rules)
- Reports of 2026 updates moving AI labels to more visible placements. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-shifts-generative-ai-labels-to-spots-viewers-will-actually-see/); [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/youtube-expands-automatic-ai-labeling-system/)
- Enforcement cases:
  - **WIRED, May 2025:** found 70+ channels posting AI-generated cartoons with violence or sexualized themes. YouTube terminated 2 channels, suspended monetization on 3, and removed videos under its Child Safety policy. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/05/02/ai-elsagate-videos-minion-go-cat-child-safety-content/); [WIRED syndicated](https://wirednews.pages.dev/story/dozens-of-youtube-channels-are-showing-ai-generated-cartoon-gore-and-fetish-content)
  - **New York Times investigation:** YouTube suspended 5 cited channels from YPP, which also removed them from YouTube Kids. Date unclear. — [Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/social-media/investigation-finds-youtube-is-serving-mindless-ai-slop-to-toddlers-and-preschoolers)
  - **Kapwing "AI slop" channel list (Nov 2025):** 16 of the listed channels were subsequently removed or wiped, 11 of them for spam and deceptive-practices violations. — [Tribune (Jan 2026)](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2589917/youtube-deletes-47-billion-views-worth-of-ai-spam-videos-in-major-platform-crackdown); [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/youtube-deletes-viral-ai-slop-channels-with-millions-of-subscribers)
  - **Advocacy pressure:** more than 200 child advocacy groups urged YouTube to ban AI slop from YouTube Kids. YouTube responded that it limits AI content in YouTube Kids to high-quality channels. — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/finance/more-200-child-advocacy-groups-023209076.html)
  - **False positives:** hand-animated channels reported being demonetized as "inauthentic". One case is Nathan Little's "Sydney the Song Cat", hand-animated over 4 years. — [Pulse (headline only; page did not load)](https://www.pulse.bot/entertainment/news/nathan-little-hand-animated-sydney-the-song-cat-for-four-years-then-youtube-demonitized-it-as-inauth-d0cd15dc-b287-4dd0-9255-580b6236815c/); [Creator Handbook: YouTube demonetizes popular animated channels](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/youtube-demonitizes-popular-animated-channels/)
- AIR's list of reasons kids channels get demonetized. — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/monetization/10-reasons-why-your-youtube-kids-channel-can-be-demonetized)

### Inferences
- An AI-assisted Arabic cartoon is monetizable if it has recurring original characters, a written story arc with a clear beginning, middle and end, clear voice acting and narration, educational or values content, and visible variety between episodes. Template-churned song or nursery-rhyme clones, keyword-stuffed titles, and knock-offs of known characters (e.g., Arabic "Elsa/Spider-Man" videos) are the highest-risk pattern.
- Because enforcement is channel-level, one batch of low-effort uploads can put the whole channel's revenue at risk.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Sydney the Song Cat was reinstated, or the exact date of the demonetization.
- I found no documented case specific to an Arabic-language AI kids channel being demonetized.

## 5. Revenue estimates for Arabic kids channels

### Takeaway
Public "earnings" figures for Arabic kids channels come from third-party estimators whose CPM assumptions vary 10x or more, so treat them as unreliable. Applying measured MFK RPMs gives a sober estimate of about $150-$900 per 1M long-form views, with roughly $330 at the median.

### Cited Findings
- **Spacetoon YouTube:** vidIQ estimates about $83K a month in AdSense, with about 13M subscribers and about 5.46B total views (2026 snapshot). — [vidIQ Spacetoon stats](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@spacetoonyoutube/); [youtubers.me](https://us.youtubers.me/spacetoon/youtuber-stats)
- **Toyor Al Janah:** NetWorthSpot estimates $267.4K a month, or about $4M a year, assuming $3-$7 CPM, which is unrealistically high for MFK. — [NetWorthSpot](https://networthspot.com/toyoraljanahtv/net-worth/)
- **Older milestone:** Spacetoon passed 2bn views and 6m subscribers on YouTube (flag: older, pre-2023). — [BroadcastPro ME](https://www.broadcastprome.com/news/spacetoon-achieves-over-2bn-views-and-6m-subscribers-on-youtube-channel/)
- Other Arabic kids channels have public stats pages: Moonbug Kids Arabic, BabyBus Arabic, Blippi Arabic, Mansour, Arabic Fairy Tales. — [vidIQ Moonbug Kids Arabic](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@moonbugkidsarabic/); [Social Blade Mansour](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/mansourcartoon-arabic); [Social Blade BabyBus AR](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/babybusar)
- **MENA creator economy (data to Dec 2024):** the number of channels earning seven figures (local currency) grew 40% YoY in Saudi Arabia, 60% in Egypt (EGP) and 15% in UAE (AED). — [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-reports-record-growth-in-mena-creator-earnings-on-20th-anniversary/)

### Inferences
Back-of-envelope estimates using AIR's MFK RPM of $0.16 / $0.33 / $0.88 (P25 / median / P75):

| Views | Low | Median | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1M long-form | ~$160 | ~$330 | ~$880 |
| 10M/month | ~$1.6K | ~$3.3K | ~$8.8K a month |
| 100M/month | ~$16K | ~$33K | ~$88K a month |

- The 100M/month estimate is consistent in magnitude with vidIQ's ~$83K a month for Spacetoon.
- Shorts at about $0.02-$0.07 RPM: 10M Shorts views earns roughly $200-$700.
- A Gulf-skewed audience could move results toward or above the high column; an Egypt or North Africa-skewed audience toward the low column.

### Gaps
- I found no audited or self-reported revenue disclosures from any Arabic kids channel.

## 6. Revenue beyond AdSense

### Takeaway
For MFK channels, the meaningful money beyond AdSense comes from:
- IP licensing to regional broadcasters and streamers (Spacetoon, Shahid/MBC, beIN's Jeem TV)
- master licensing and merchandise deals, as Spacetoon has done with YouTube-native brands
- multi-language dubbing, to reach higher-RPM audiences

Sponsorship and paid placement is constrained: paid placements get content removed from YouTube Kids, and ads must not target kids' purchasing.

### Cited Findings
- **Licensing to regional platforms:**
  - Spacetoon partnered with the Arab family YouTube channel The Moshaya Family to build a franchise (toys, animated series, games), including a toy line with Toy Pro in MENA. — [Kidscreen "Planetary growth"](https://kidscreen.com/?p=82354)
  - Spacetoon signed a licensing deal with Pocket.watch to extend kids YouTube stars into MENA. — [BroadcastPro ME](https://broadcastprome.com/news/spacetoon-signs-deal-with-pocket-watch-to-broadcast-kids-shows-in-mena)
  - Spacetoon is master licensee for CoComelon in MENA, through a partnership with Moonbug. — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/spacetoon-partners-with-moonbug-entertainment-to-bring-preschool-sensation-cocomelon-and-five-other-audience-favorites-to-mena/); [Spacetoon Group](https://spacetoongroup.com/news/spacetoon-and-moonbug-entertainment-extend-partnership)
  - Spacetoon has a co-production deal with Toikido (3 shows) and an equity stake in Samaka Studio for IP creation. — [AWN](https://awn.com/tag/spacetoon); [World Screen](https://worldscreen.com/tvkids/?p=30766)
  - Spacetoon's live channel is on MBC's Shahid. — [BroadcastPro ME](https://broadcastprome.com/news/spacetoon-and-shahid-embark-on-live-streaming-partnership)
  - Pinkfong licensed about 500 Baby Shark episodes, dubbed in Arabic, to Shahid (older, about 2021). — [Kidscreen](https://kidscreen.com/?p=194338)
  - Barajoun Entertainment and Shahid have announced Arabic animation plans. — [Cartoon Brew](https://cartoonbrew.com/tag/barajoun-entertainment)
  - In Sept 2026, Union Media (Beirut) licensed "Science Voyagers" (30x10') to beIN for Jeem TV. — [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/?p=292600)
  - WildBrain CPLG holds MENA licensing and merchandising rights for the Emirati series "Mansour". — [Licensing International](https://licensinginternational.org/news/wildbrain-cplg-secures-exclusive-licensing-merchandising-rights-to-animated-kids-series-mansour-in-the-middle-east-and-north-africa/)
- **Multi-language audio:**
  - YouTube expanded multi-language audio to "millions of creators" in Sept 2025. Creators using it saw over 25% of watch time from non-primary-language views. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/youtube-launches-multi-language-audio-for-millions-of-creators-extending-global-reach-with-auto-dubbing-493458-2025-09-11)
  - AI auto-dubbing has expanded to 27 languages and to all users (Feb 2026). — [India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/youtube-auto-dubbing-feature-expands-to-all-users-now-supports-27-languages-2026-02-05-1029070); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/youtube-expands-ai-auto-dubbing-to-27-languages-with-expressive-speech-1.500433271)
- **Sponsorship rules:**
  - Videos flagged as containing paid product placement or endorsement are removed from the YouTube Kids app, and disclosure is required. — [YouTube Help: paid promotion for kids & families](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10502576); [Toybook](https://toybook.com/youtube-kids-to-remove-overly-commercial-content-paid-placements/)
  - "Heavily promotional" content is a low-quality signal under the kids principles. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223)
  - Current branded content policy page. — [YouTube Branded Content Policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17596007?hl=en)
- **Merch and fan funding:** the YouTube merch shelf, memberships, Super Chat and donate buttons are disabled on MFK content, so merchandise must be sold off-platform. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654)

### Inferences
- The most valuable strategic asset is owning the IP (characters, songs, scripts) so it can be licensed to Spacetoon, Shahid or Jeem and to toy makers. YouTube serves as the audience-proof and discovery channel rather than the main revenue source. Spacetoon's deals with YouTube-native brands (Moshaya, Pocket.watch, Moonbug) show the regional buyer appetite.
- Dubbing an Arabic cartoon into English, French, Indonesian or Turkish (or the reverse) can access audiences with higher RPMs than the blended Arab average. Note that English-speaking US kids audiences are also MFK, so the RPM is still low.
- Further options: apps (subscription or ad-free), books and activity books, audio/podcast platforms, and Patreon aimed at parents. These must be marketed to parents off-platform, because MFK disables links, cards and comments.
- Selling or licensing episodes to other channels or aggregators (e.g., Moonbug-style networks) is another route, but no Arabic-specific deal terms were found.

### Gaps
- No public deal values (license fees per episode or per minute) were found for Shahid, OSN+, Spacetoon or Jeem acquisitions.
- I could not confirm whether Arabic is among the 27 auto-dubbing languages.
- I found no data on Patreon, app or book revenue for Arabic kids brands.
- OSN+ kids acquisition practices were not found.

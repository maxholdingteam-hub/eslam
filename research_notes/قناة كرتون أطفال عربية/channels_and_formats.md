# Arabic Kids Animation YouTube Channels & Winning Formats (state as of Nov 2026)

Data caveat: subscriber/view figures come from third-party trackers (vidIQ, Social Blade, SocialCounts). These are public estimates with differing snapshot dates; vidIQ pages fetched on 2026-11-13 are the most current. YouTube rounds subscriber counts (3 significant figures), so "0 change in 28 days" on vidIQ often just means growth below the rounding step, not true stagnation.

## 1. Which Arabic kids channels lead in 2025-2026 and what format do they use?

### Takeaway
The Arabic kids category is dominated by two long-established, music-first brands from the same Jordan/Palestine-origin studio ecosystem: Toyor Aljanah (~33.8M subs, ~32.2B views) and Karameesh (~17.7M subs, ~14.3B views). Both get over 110M views a month mainly from Arabic children's songs that feature recurring child performers or characters. Arabic dubs of global brands (Cocomelon Arabic, Moonbug Kids Arabic) sit far below, in the 1-1.5M subscriber tier. Original educational/Islamic channels mostly sit between ~100K and ~1.7M subscribers.

Top videos are songs sung by the Miqdad family children: "سنّو عم يطلع" 157M views (2022), "شاور شاور (الباقة الكاملة)" 114.7M (a full-package compilation), "هابي بيرث دي" 72.6M, "ع البانيو (بدون إيقاع)" 72.5M (a no-percussion version), "جوعانين" 68.5M. — [vidIQ, data 2026-11-13](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@toyoraljanahtv/)

### Inferences
- The scale gap is huge. Toyor Aljanah plus Karameesh get ~245M views/month combined, versus ~13M for Cocomelon Arabic and ~2M for Moonbug Arabic. Native Arabic song brands with their own characters and child singers far outperform dubbed global IP.
- Toyor Aljanah's top titles are in Levantine colloquial ("سنّو عم يطلع", "جوعانين", "ع البانيو"), and its biggest hits are about everyday routines: teething, bath, shower, hunger, birthday. This is the same routine-song formula as Cocomelon's top hits (potty, healthy habits, bath). Toddler-routine songs in simple, partly dialect lyrics appear to be the single strongest Arabic format. (This inference is based on titles only.)
- Fusha appears dominant among educational and Islamic channels (Learn, Kalam Kids, Rayan). Dubbed brands typically use Fusha for pan-Arab reach. No tracker measures dialect versus Fusha directly.

### Gaps
- Social Blade and HypeAuditor rank pages were blocked (403) or not kids-specific. The HypeAuditor "Animation – Saudi Arabia" list is mostly gamers, so it is not usable. There is no authoritative "Arabic kids" ranking.
- Current 2026 figures for Adam Wa Mishmish, PaRaRa Arabic, Learn, Omar & Hana, Super Simple Arabic and Pinkfong Arabic were not fetched.
- No data on Arabic dubs of Peppa Pig, Masha, Pinkfong/Baby Shark or Bluey on YouTube.

## 2. Which formats drive the most views?

### Takeaway
On the evidence available, the order is: (1) original music videos and nursery/routine songs with recurring characters, (2) long "full package" compilations of those songs, (3) dubbed global preschool IP, and (4) educational letters/numbers/Islamic-basics content. Dubbed story cartoons with no strong IP do poorly. Islamic kids content has a dedicated niche but no channel near the top tier was found.

### Cited Findings
- Toyor Aljanah's #2 all-time video is a compilation ("الباقة الكاملة", 114.7M). It also uploads "بدون إيقاع" (no percussion/instruments) versions, its #4 video at 72.5M. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@toyoraljanahtv/)
- Cocomelon Arabic's top 5 videos are all song adaptations (routine, nursery rhyme and health songs). — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@cocomelonarabic/)
- Dubbed fairy-tale and shapes cartoons on Kids tv Arabic peak at ~1.8M views per video despite 1.67M subs. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UC9ya8rlHpqsS3yIAqs55PeA)
- **Global benchmark:** Cocomelon has 203M subs, 229.7B views, 2,182 videos and ~1.36-1.41B views per month (Aug-Oct 2026). Its top videos are all songs: Humpty Dumpty Grocery Store 1.57B, Yes Yes Fruits 1.23B, Twinkle Twinkle 877M. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@cocomelon/)
- An analysis of Cocomelon finds most of its total video hours come from compilations or reuse of a relatively small pool of original material. — [RPubs analysis](https://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/1333544_e645aacb5a7045699bab249a86ac4d10.html)
- An AIR Media-Tech case study (vendor, promotional) of a million-subscriber kids channel reports that compilations raised watch duration +19%, CPM +9% and RPM +72%. Views rose +27% after launching Shorts. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/creators-spotlight/268-view-growth-for-a-million-subscriber-kids-channel-case-study)
- AIR also reports that an Arabic kids channel's recommendation-feed traffic "exploded" after AI-translated metadata (titles/descriptions in multiple languages). No absolute numbers are given and the source is promotional. — [AIR](https://air.io/en/creators-spotlight/what-kids-channel-did-with-metadata-content-growth)

### Inferences
- Songs are rewatchable, which is what drives the Cocomelon and Toyor-scale numbers; one-off stories are watched once. For a new Arabic channel, a song-led core with character stories as a secondary strand is the most evidence-backed mix.
- "No-music/no-percussion" versions are a distinctly Arabic/Islamic-market format. They serve conservative parents who avoid instruments and are proven at the 70M+ view level. This is a low-cost variant to add to every song.

### Gaps
- No platform-level data split views by format (songs vs stories vs Shorts) for Arabic kids content.
- No figures found for leading Islamic-kids animation channels (e.g., Omar & Hana, Zaky/One4Kids).

## 3. Video length, cadence, compilations, thumbnails/titles

### Takeaway
Leaders run a large back catalogue: 950 to 3,600 videos. Average cadence is roughly 10-20 uploads/month historically. Single songs (2-4 min) are repackaged into long compilations. YouTube's 2025 algorithm shift cut the payoff of ultra-long (>2-3h) videos, so 30-60 minute compilations look like the safer default.

### Cited Findings
- Video counts: Toyor 3,615 (since 2008), Karameesh 2,851 (since 2012), Cocomelon 2,182, Moonbug Arabic 1,551, Kids tv Arabic 1,381, Cocomelon Arabic 944 (since 2021). — vidIQ pages cited above. Implied average cadence: Karameesh ~16/month and Cocomelon Arabic ~14/month (calculated from count divided by age).
- **Little Dot Studios (800+ channels, 11.2B monthly views):** in 2023-24, ultra-long (>120 min) videos over-delivered, and a 120-min video earned about 100x the revenue of a 20-min one. A January 2025 algorithm update cut ultra-long viewing by more than 90% within three months. The average length of recommended videos fell from ~80 to ~40 min, and the most popular videos' average length fell 21% (~35 to ~28 min, Dec 2024 to May 2025). By 2025, views per video were roughly even across short, long and ultra-long. Viewing is increasingly on living-room TVs and driven by the algorithm rather than subscriptions. — [Tubefilter on Little Dot whitepaper, Feb 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/02/12/little-dot-youtube-video-length-whitepaper-viewer-habits-2026/amp/)
- **Metricool 2026 study:** long-form views rose 76% and Shorts views 127% year over year, while average watch time fell. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/). Since Mar 31, 2025, every Shorts start or replay counts as a view. — [AIR Shorts vs long](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/should-you-chase-shorts-views-or-double-down-on-long-form-for-channel-growth)
- **Title patterns seen in top Arabic videos:** "song name – singer names | channel brand" (e.g., "سنّو عم يطلع - سند مقداد | طيور الجنة"), plus variant tags such as "(الباقة الكاملة)" and "(بدون إيقاع)". — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@toyoraljanahtv/)

### Inferences
- For a new channel, a reasonable cadence is 2-4 new songs/episodes per week, plus 1-2 compilations (30-60 min) per week built from the back catalogue, plus Shorts cut from each song.
- Kids titles should lead with the song or topic in simple Arabic and end with the brand. Thumbnails likely benefit from one big character face in bright, saturated colour (standard practice, not verified with data here).

### Gaps
- Actual average video durations and the long-form vs Shorts split for Arabic channels were not exposed in the fetched vidIQ text.
- No thumbnail A/B data for Arabic kids content.

## 4. Growth of newer (2023-2025) and AI-assisted Arabic kids channels

### Takeaway
No well-documented, verifiable case of a 2023-2025 AI-generated Arabic kids cartoon channel with published growth figures was found. Globally, AI "educational" toddler channels are proliferating and drawing criticism. The opportunity is real, but so are the quality, safety and reputational risks.

### Cited Findings
- A Bloomberg report (Dec 2025, summarised by YPulse) says creators are using AI to mass-produce colourful "educational" toddler content and can earn "hundreds of dollars a day". Revenue scales with posting frequency. YouTube use rose more among under-2s than any other age group over five years, and over 60% of parents say their infant or toddler watches YouTube daily. Experts warn of risks to language and emotional development. AI videos on YouTube Kids are "nearly indistinguishable" from vetted content. — [YPulse](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2025/12/15/youtube-channels-are-targeting-the-youngest-audience-with-ai-content/)
- A report (Aug 2026) describes disturbing AI-generated cartoons infiltrating YouTube's kids ecosystem. — [CO/AI](https://getcoai.com/news/monster-cats-gnarly-minions-ai-generated-cartoon-gore-floods-youtube-channels/)
- BabaSharo TV says it is developing a "fully AI-powered" children's project (self-reported press release). — [GetNews](https://markets.financialcontent.com/wral/article/getnews-2025-7-19-babasharo-tv-hits-18-billion-views-the-arabic-creator-behind-one-of-youtubes-top-kids-channels)
- The Fixies passed 3M subs (Feb 2026), and its Arabic channel reportedly showed "particularly rapid momentum in MENA" (not AI). — [Senal News](https://senalnews.com/en/content/the-fixies-hit-3-million-subscribers-on-youtube-as-global-reach-continues-to-grow)
- Cocomelon Arabic (launched 2021) reached 1.52M subs in ~5.5 years despite a global brand behind it. This is a benchmark for how slowly even strong IP grows in Arabic. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@cocomelonarabic/)

### Inferences
- Even well-funded entrants plateau around 1-1.7M subs. Breaking into the Toyor/Karameesh tier took 10+ years and an owned music and character brand. Realistic expectations for a new AI-assisted channel are tens to a few hundred thousand subs in year one, and only if it consistently ships quality songs.
- AI can lower animation cost, but a distinctive, consistent character design and a human-quality Arabic voice and music track are where differentiation will be judged by parents and YouTube's "made for kids" quality principles.

### Gaps
- No Social Blade growth curves were obtainable (403) for 2023-2025 Arabic kids channels.
- No named Arabic AI kids channel with verified metrics was found. A dedicated search on vidIQ/Playboard "rising channels" for Arabic kids would be needed.

## 5. Underserved niches / gaps

### Takeaway
The evidence points to gaps in fresh, high-quality original Arabic IP beyond the two incumbents. The specific gaps are Gulf/Egyptian-dialect toddler content, music-free (Islamic-friendly) song libraries, Arabic letters and phonics with strong characters, and preschool story series with Islamic/Arab values made to modern animation standards.

### Cited Findings
- Dubbed generic content (Kids tv Arabic, Moonbug Arabic) has large subscriber bases but weak current monthly views (~2M), which suggests audience demand for native-feeling content. — vidIQ pages above.
- The biggest Arabic hits are Levantine-dialect songs by one studio (Toyor/Karameesh). No Gulf- or Egyptian-dialect animated kids brand appeared in any ranking fetched. — [vidIQ Toyor](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@toyoraljanahtv/); [HypeAuditor Saudi animation list](https://hypeauditor.com/top-youtube-animation-saudi-arabia) (no kids animation in the Saudi top 100).
- The "بدون إيقاع" demand is proven (72.5M views on one video). — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@toyoraljanahtv/)
- Educational and Islamic channels (Learn 1.56M, Kalam Kids 254K, Rayan 268K) show demand but have no dominant animated leader. — vidIQ pages above.

### Inferences
- Candidate niches: (a) Fusha phonics and letters songs with a recurring animated character (a "Cocomelon for الحروف" style); (b) a no-instrument nasheed library for toddlers; (c) Gulf-dialect daily-routine songs for the Saudi/UAE market (high CPM); (d) short moral/Islamic-values stories (5-8 min) bundled into 30-60 minute compilations; (e) Arabic STEM/curiosity series for ages 5-8, which are largely absent from the rankings.

### Gaps
- No survey data on MENA parents' preferences (Fusha vs dialect, music vs no music).
- No CPM data by MENA country for kids content was found in this research.

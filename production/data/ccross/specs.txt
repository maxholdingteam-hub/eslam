C. PER-GRADE SUMMARY
=======================================================================
LE HEV - SAR 103,845
  Engine: 1.8L 4-cyl hybrid, 121 HP combined, CVT, FWD, 25.8 km/L, 17" alloys
  Lights: halogen headlights, DRL
  Seats: fabric; 4.2" multi-information display; 8" touchscreen; Apple CarPlay; USB-C; 6 speakers
  Safety: 2 airbags; rear sensors + rear camera; ABS/VSC/HAC/TPWS/EPB
XLE HEV - SAR 113,505  (= LE plus:)
  + LED front fog lamps, roof rails, moon roof, front parking sensors, tonneau cover,
    sun visors with ILLUMINATED vanity mirrors
  Still: halogen headlights, fabric seats, 4.2" MID, 8" screen, 2 airbags
Limited HEV - SAR 129,260  (= XLE plus:)
  + LED headlamps, LED DRL, LED rear lamps
  + leather seats, leather steering wheel, 12.3" multi-information display (features tab: "12.3-inch Full Graphic
    Display Meter"), 10" touchscreen, wireless charger, interior blue ambient lighting
  + power back door, BSM, RCTA, 7 airbags
Limited Plus HEV - SAR 130,410
  Site text and compare table list exactly the Limited HEV content EXCEPT "Wireless Charger", which is missing for
  Limited Plus in both EN and AR. No feature is listed that Limited Plus has and Limited lacks.
  The colour selector (incl. the 4 black-roof two-tone colours) is attached only to Limited Plus HEV.
  -> What Limited Plus adds for the extra SAR 1,150: NOT ON SITE (possibly the two-tone roof, but the site does not
     say so; the missing wireless charger may be a site error - confirm with ALJ before advertising).

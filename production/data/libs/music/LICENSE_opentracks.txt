Summary for the Hilux ad (tracks ot_13971, ot_20880, ot_9389, ot_9922, ot_12426, ot_23035):
  - Free; commercial and corporate use allowed; NO credit / copyright notice required.
  - "Use in TV programs and commercials" and online video are listed as allowed background-music uses
    (so, unlike Mixkit, a TV/broadcast version of the ad is also covered).
  - Editing (cutting, looping, fades, effects) allowed.
  - Downloaded with the public download button on each track page - no account.
  - Prohibited: political / religious / ideological content; using the music as the main content; redistributing or
    selling the audio itself; registering it with Content ID / any fingerprinting system or with JASRAC etc.;
    AI training; uses that damage the composer's reputation.
  - Composer-specific terms take precedence where they exist: all downloaded tracks are marked "サイト準拠"
    (site-compliant = no extra conditions). Yosame Yu (13971) asks for a credit "if possible" (optional).
  - Japanese law governs; the site gives no warranty and answers no individual permission requests.

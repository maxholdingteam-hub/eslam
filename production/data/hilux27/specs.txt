0. SUMMARY (read this first)
==========================================================================================
 * The site sells "Hilux 2027" = the all-new generation (home-page banner: "LEGEND HILUX - The All-New 2027" /
   "الأسطورة HILUX - 2027 الجديدة كلياً"). 10 grades on one page: 7 DOUBLE CAB (DC) + 3 SINGLE CAB (SC). ALL are 4x4.
 * 3 ENGINES, 2 FUEL TYPES (petrol + diesel). NO hybrid, NO 48V, NO electric anywhere on the KSA page.
     - 2.7L 4-cyl petrol VVT-i   164 HP / 5200 rpm, 245 NM / 4000 rpm      -> DC-GL (5MT), DC-SGLX (6AT), SC-GL (5MT)
     - 4.0L 6-cyl petrol VVT-i   235 HP / 5200 rpm, 376 NM / 3800 rpm      -> DC-Premium (6AT) ONLY
     - 2.8L 4-cyl turbo diesel   201 HP, 420 NM (6MT) / 500 NM (6AT)        -> DC-GL DSL (MT/AT), DC-GLX DSL, DC-SGLX DSL, SC-GL DSL (MT/AT)
   Engine codes named on the site (feature card "Engine Line-Up"): 1GD-FTV (2.8 turbo diesel), 1GR-FE and 2TR-FE (petrol; the site
   does not say which code is which displacement). The site never writes "V6"; it says "6 cylinders".
 * DC prices (SAR, incl. 15% VAT and shipping fee, excl. registration + insurance):
     DC-GL 2.7 MT 125,867.50 | DC-SGLX 2.7 AT 157,032.50 | DC-Premium 4.0 AT 179,515 | DC-GL DSL MT 133,515 |
     DC-GL DSL AT 140,242.50 | DC-GLX DSL AT 159,447.50 | DC-SGLX DSL AT 179,687.50 (the most expensive grade).
 * HERO LOOK = DC-PREMIUM 4.0L (petrol, 4.0L 6-cyl, 235 HP, 6AT). Evidence: (a) the studio/gallery white car carries a black "PREMIUM"
   tailgate emblem (spec: 'Model Black Emblem "Premium"' exists only on DC-Premium); (b) every exterior photo (hero, gallery, cutouts,
   key visual) shows black over-fender mouldings - "Over Fender Moulding" is listed ONLY for DC-Premium; (c) the colour selector /
   all 7 colour cutouts exist ONLY under "DC- Premium 4.0L - 4X4 AT" ("Double Cap Premium 4.0L - 4X4 AT"); (d) 18in diamond-cut/gloss-black
   wheels (Premium/SGLX only). The page hero itself shows no grade badge (front 3/4), but it is the same over-fender/18in body.
 * RECOMMENDATION: build the ad on the DC-Premium 4.0L PETROL (4.0L 6-cylinder, 235 HP / 376 NM, 6-speed automatic, 4x4, SAR 179,515).
   End "versions" scene: the other engine types are the 2.8L TURBO DIESEL (201 HP; 420 NM manual / 500 NM automatic; GL DSL MT/AT,
   GLX DSL, SGLX DSL) and the 2.7L 4-cyl PETROL (164 HP; GL MT, SGLX AT). Optionally mention "also available as Single Cab".
   Caution: do NOT pair the Premium with the site's engine photo (it shows a 4-cylinder turbo engine = the diesel type), nor with
   Multi-Terrain Select / DAC / auto stop-start claims (diesel GLX/SGLX only). No TSS, no 7 airbags, no hybrid - see sec. 6.

6. SAFETY SUMMARY
==========================================================================================
 - Airbags: every grade "Airbags: Driver - Driver Knee - Front Passenger" (AR "وسائد هوائية: للسائق - ركبة السائق - الراكب الأمامي"; CT "Driver + Knee &
   Front Passenger"). = 3 positions. The site prints no airbag count and mentions NO side/curtain airbags.
 - Toyota Safety Sense: NOT mentioned on any grade (no PCS, lane, adaptive cruise, BSM, RCTA). Cruise Control is conventional (D2 D3 D6 D7).
 - All grades: ABS, EBD, VSC, immobilizer, TPWS (GL) / TPMS (D2 D3 D6 D7). HAC / TRC are not mentioned anywhere.
 - DAC (Down-hill Assist Control): D6 D7 only. EPB + Brake Hold: D2 D3 D7. Front+rear disc brakes: D2 D3 D7 (others front disc / rear drum).
 - Cameras: Panoramic View Monitor (4 Cameras) D3 only; Rear Camera D2 D6 D7; none on GL. Front corner sensors D2 D3 D7; rear sensors all (dealer-fitted* on GL/GLX).
 - Dealer kit: "Safety Package (Made in China)*" (all but Premium) / "Safety Regulation Kit (Made in China)*" (Premium) - AR "حقيبة السلامة" / "حقيبة السلامة الأساسية".
 - Do NOT claim TSS, 7 airbags, curtain airbags, hybrid or towing figures - none are on the KSA site.
